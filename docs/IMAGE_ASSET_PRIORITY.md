# Image Asset Priority

Phase 8 recommendation: keep generated art focused and sparse. The exhibit already works with CSS/SVG placeholders, so future Image 2 assets should strengthen the tactile museum mood without turning the interface into a decorative collage.

Shared Image 2 style direction:

> Whimsical 2.5D archival exhibit object, warm off-white newsprint texture, JMU-inspired purple and gold, Harrisonburg college-town civic mood, playful but not childish, public museum touchscreen exhibit, soft shadows, no logos, no readable text baked into the image.

## 1. The Breeze Parade Background / Market Scene

- Purpose: Make the attractor mode feel more like a living civic poster than a CSS mockup.
- Where used: The Breeze Parade bottom scene in landscape and portrait.
- Transparent background: No.
- Recommended dimensions: 3840 x 1400 for landscape source, with safe crop for portrait.
- Image 2 prompt draft: Whimsical 2.5D Harrisonburg college-town parade and market street, storefront silhouettes, crosswalk, newspaper boxes, campus bus shape, warm newsprint texture, JMU-inspired purple and gold accents, public museum touchscreen exhibit, no logos, no readable text.
- Why it improves the exhibit: This is the first screen visitors see, so a stronger scene would increase invitation and memory-lane atmosphere.
- Risk if overused: Too much scenic detail could compete with the title, clipping card, and entry button.
